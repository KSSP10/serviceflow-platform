# Scope (Day 1)

## MVP acceptance (from PRD)
- Tenant admin can sign in and manage users.
- CSR can create customer, location, and job from one flow.
- Dispatcher can assign and reschedule jobs on a visual board.
- Calendar supports exact times, arrival windows, and conflict handling.
- Technician workflow supports status progression and invoice completion.
- Pricebook, estimate, invoice, and payment flows work end to end.
- Notification emails are sent for key events.
- AI can summarize intake and suggest next actions (prototype acceptable).

## Explicit exclusions (Non-goals for MVP)
- Full payroll and accounting general ledger.
- Native telephony platform.
- Advanced marketing attribution.
- Complex multi-day construction project management.
